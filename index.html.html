Questions, answered

Is the trial really free?

Yes. You get 7 days of free classes. No payment details are needed to start.

How does weekly payment work?

After the trial you pay for one week at a time, at $10, $15 or $20 depending on your plan. You can pause or cancel before the next week begins.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Move up or down at the start of any week.

What do we need for classes?

A phone, tablet or computer with internet, and a quiet place to recite.

Do you teach adults and children?

Yes, from age 4 to adults, with separate teaching methods for each.
